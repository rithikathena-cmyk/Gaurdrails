COOPERATIVE DEPARTMENT 
 
CITIZEN CHARTER 
 
INTRODUCTION: 
Cooperatives all over the world have become an effective and potential 
instrument of economic development. The Cooperative Movement in Tamil Nadu 
has witnessed substantial growth in diverse areas of economy. There is not a single 
major sphere of economic activity which has not been touched by Cooperatives. 
Cooperatives are also envisaged as an instrument for implementing many important 
policies like agricultural credit, urban credit, market intervention, price support for 
agricultural commodities through Cooperative Wholesale stores, Public Distribution 
system etc.,  
From a small beginning, the Cooperative movement in Tamil Nadu has grown 
in strength over the years. Opening of a Village Cooperative credit society in 
Thiroor in Thiruvalur District in 1904 marked the advent of the Cooperative 
movement in Tamil Nadu ushering in a new era of services for the people of the 
state, particularly the agriculturist families. From Agricultural Banks to Marketing 
societies and Consumer Cooperatives provide service to the people in various 
economic activities. Cooperatives also run the Public Distribution system which 
provides relief to each and every family in the state. 
 
1. PRINCIPLES OF COOPERATION 
a) Open membership. 
b)  Democratic management. 
c)  Limited interest on capital. 
d)  Distribution of surplus based on patronage. 
e)  Provision of Co-operative Education and Training. 
f)  Co-operation among Cooperatives for the promotion of thrift, self help and 
mutual aid among persons with common socio-economic needs. 
g) Concern for Community

2 
 
2. DUTIES OF CITIZENS 
Every member should repay his dues to co-operative society in time. 
Every member should exercise his vote at General Body Meeting. 
Every member should participate in the General Body activities. 
Every member should involve himself in the society activities for the welfare of 
the society, subject to the bylaw of the society concerned. 
 
3. ACT, RULES AND BY-LAWS : 
The Co-operative Societies functioning in Tamil Nadu are governed by the 
following : 
(i) 
Tamil Nadu Co-operative Societies Act, 1983 
(ii) 
Tamil Nadu Co-operative Societies Rules, 1988 
(iii) 
By-laws of the respective institutions

COOPERATIVE DEPARTMENT 
Administrative Set up 
 
Cooperative Department under Government of Tamil Nadu is headed by the 
Hon’ble Minister for Cooperation. Cooperative Department is functioning under the 
supervision of the Principal Secretary to Government, Cooperation, Food and 
Consumer Protection Department. Registrar of Cooperative Societies in the cadre of 
I.A.S., is the Head of the Cooperative Department. At the Headquarters, the 
Registrar of Cooperative Societies is assisted by One Additional Registrar in the 
cadre of I.A.S., 4 Additional Registrars, 4 Joint Registrars and 11 Deputy Registrars 
on functional basis.  
 
There are thirty two regional offices in the State with a Joint Registrar of 
Cooperative Societies in charge of each region (District) except Chennai.   The 
Additional Registrar of Cooperative Societies is the Regional Head so far as Chennai 
is concerned. The Cooperative department has been divided into 73 Circles for the 
proper administration of the department.  The Circles are on the territorial basis co-
terminus to Revenue Division.  In Chennai City, there are two circles which are 
functioning on functional basis, one for credit and the other for non-credit 
cooperatives.   
           In every district, there is one Deputy Registrar (Public Distribution System) 
with staff for the supervision of Fair Price Shops functioning in the districts.  They 
are also working under the direct control of the Regional Joint Registrars except 
Chennai.   
There are two Joint Registrar of Cooperative Societies (Public Distribution 
System) in Chennai region, exclusively to monitor and to attend the work relating 
to distribution of essential commodities through the Public Distribution System 
outlets of the cooperative societies functioning in Chennai City and belt areas. 
There are seven Deputy Registrars (Public Distribution System) in Chennai City 
functioning under the direct control of Joint Registrars (Public Distribution System), 
Chennai.

COOPERATIVE CREDIT INSTITUTIONS 
 
OBJECTIVE : 
The Cooperative Credit institutions play a vital role in 
disbursing loans at reasonable rate of interest in a specified 
time. Because of this, the farmers, artisans, entrepreneurs, 
differently abled and weaker section are prevented from 
relying on money lenders who offer loans at higher rate of 
interest. 
 
Structure:   
a) Short Term Cooperative Credit structure comprises of  three tiers: 
 
1) Tamil Nadu State Apex Cooperative Bank  
2) District Central Cooperative Banks 
3) Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Societies. 
 
1) Tamil Nadu State Apex Cooperative Bank , Chennai 
 
Tamil Nadu State Apex Cooperative Bank started functioning in the year 
1905 and at present, the bank is functioning with 51 branches, an extension 
counter. 
 
Functions: 
➢ Tamil Nadu State Apex Cooperative Bank plays an important role in 
disbursement of Loans to the Cooperative Institutions in Tamil Nadu. 
 
➢ Provides finance to Central Cooperative Banks to disburse short and 
medium term loans to farmers. 
 
➢ Provides finance through Central Cooperative Banks to improve the 
livelihood of farmers and rural people by way of providing non farm 
sector loans.

➢ Credit facility is being extended through Central Cooperative Banks to

Consumer 
Cooperative 
Wholesale 
Stores, 
Cooperative 
Marketing 
Societies, Primary Cooperative Stores, Primary Agricultural Cooperative 
Credit Societies.

➢ Tamil Nadu State Apex Cooperative Bank also provides finance directly to

other Apex Cooperative institutions.

➢ Jewel Loans, Small Industry loan, and other banking facilities to the

population of Chennai city.

ELIGIBILITY TO OBTAIN LOAN 
 
➢ The applicant should be above 18 years. 
➢ Should reside in the operational area of the bank (or) work in the 
operational area. 
➢ Should be a Associate member and has to pay entrance fees. 
➢ Should be a customer of the bank (or) should have opened a new bank 
account. 
➢ Should submit the necessary documents (All KYC documents e.g., Aadhar, 
PAN) 
➢ Should satisfy the regulations for the loan which applied for.

Person whom should be approached for availing the loan

To avail loan in the Central Cooperative Banks one should approach the 
Branch Manager in that particular area.

➢ The Prime objective of these societies is issuing crop loans to farmers in

public and Special Cash Credit loan obtained from the Central Cooperative 
Bank. 
 
➢ Deposit mobilization, sale of fertilizer and seeds are also being undertaken 
by these societies.

E-Sevai  Centres 
 
There are 4,448 E-Sevai Centres functioning in Primary Agricultural Co-
operative Societies and other Co-operative Institutions throughout Tamil Nadu with 
the main objective of providing Government services to the public, especially the 
rural people in remote areas. E-Sevai Centres provide income services such as 
Community Certificate, Income Certificate, Birth / Death Certificate, Patta / Chitta 
and other certificates. It provides service to avail many welfare schemes such as Girl 
Child Protection Scheme and Marriage Assistance Scheme provided by the Social 
Welfare Department. It also provides services of paying various taxes and fees, like 
electricity bill, urban property tax, drinking water tax and sewage water disposal fee. 
In addition to these services, the Centres also offer a number of other private 
services such as life insurance premium payments and electronic ticket booking. 
Thus, various services are provided at one place in a timely manner, which reduces 
the time and cost for the public who have to otherwise approach various Government 
Offices. Transparency of services has increased the credibility among the common 
public.

National Food Security Act: 
    
The Goverment of Tamilnadu has implemented National Food Security Act, 
2013 in the state with effect from 01.11.2016 without affecting the features of 
Universal Public Distribution System being implemented in the State. As per this 
act, all rice cardholders are eligible for 5 kg of rice per person (irrespective of adult 
or children) per month and there is no upper ceiling of 20 kg per card per month. 
Government have not effected any changes in supplying 12 Kg to single unit cards,   
16 Kg to two unit cards and 35 Kg to Antodhaya Anna Yojana (AAY) Cards.

cardholders in Chennai City, Belt area and district headquarters and 5 Kg of Wheat 
is being supplied in other areas at free of cost.

Linking of Consumer Cooperatives with Marketing  
 
❖ Cooperative Marketing Societies are working with tie-up of Cooperative 
Whole sale stores. 
❖ By procuring the farmers produce, processing it and selling it through 
Cooperative Whole Sale Stores (by Agricultural Producers Cooperative 
Marketing Societies) consumers are able to get quality products at 
reasonable price and the farmers in turn get a remunerative price for the 
produce. 
 
(iv) Linking of Credit with Marketing  
  
❖ The members of Primary Agricultural cooperative Credit Societies are 
enrolled as the members of Agricultural Producers Cooperative Marketing 
Societies. 
❖ The Agricultural Producers Cooperative Marketing Societies assist in the 
sales of their Agricultural produce. 
❖ These efforts help the Primary Agricultural cooperative Credit Societies to 
recover loans besides assisting the farmer members in selling their produce 
at higher price. 
 
Procurement of Copra under Price Support Scheme (PSS) 
❖ Whenever market price of copra is low, copra procurement will be 
undertaken at the Minimum Support Price by Tamil Nadu Cooperative 
Marketing Federation by acting as a State level agency on behalf of NAFED.

Paddy Procurement under Minimum Support Price (MSP)

❖ Whenever market price of Paddy is low, Agricultural Producers Cooperative

Marketing Societies will procure Paddy from farmers on behalf of the Tamil

Nadu Civil Supplies Corporation in non delta areas at the Minimum Support

Price.

❖ To avoid additional expenses to farmers, paddy is procured through

cooperatives nearby.

Cooperative Printing Press 
 
Structure: 
 In Tamil Nadu, 26 Cooperative Printing Presses are functioning with a total 
membership of 11,991 and a paid up share capital amount of Rs.2.72 crore  
Objectives:- 
➢ Cooperative Printing Presses were formed with the objective of fulfilling the 
Printing needs of Cooperatives.  
➢ The main objective of Cooperative Printing Presses is to undertake the 
printing works and binding works of Cooperative institutions, Government 
office, Local bodies and General public. 
 
Services:- 
➢ Cooperative Printing Presses are fulfilling the printing needs of  Cooperative 
societies, Government institutions, Private institutions and General public 
within its area of operation. 
➢ The Cooperative Printing Presses undertake Printing Receipt forms, Bill 
books, Registers, Calendars & Diaries and binding works. 
 
Guidelines for getting printing services 
➢ Customers should approach the concerned Printing Press Official or 
employees for placing orders for their printing requirement. 
➢ The compositor should give a copy of the proof to the customer. 
➢  Printing work will be done after verifying the proof by the customer. 
➢ For undertaking printing order Minimum 200 numbers of copies have to be 
given. 
➢ The completed printing works will be supplied at the customer door step. 
➢ In order to compete with the private printing presses, Chennai, Vellore, 
Trichy, Salem, Coimbatore, Madurai and Tirunelveli Cooperative Printing

presses were modernized by installing modern machineries, able to print 
Computer Stationery, and other printing needs. Further, Ramanathapuram, 
Thanjavur, Namakkal, virudhunagar, cuddalore and Tiruvannamalai district 
Cooperative Printing Presses were also modernized. Out of 26 Cooperative 
Printing Presses, 13 Cooperative Printing Presses are modernized and they 
are undertaking the printing needs of the customers.

        To popularize Cooperative Principles and their benefits to people, Tamil Nadu 
Cooperative Union at State level and 38 District Cooperative Unions at District level 
are functioning.

• 
Imparting Cooperative Education, Training, Advertisement and propaganda 
work have been carried out through Tamil Nadu Cooperative Union and 
District Cooperative Union. 
• 
Member Education Programme, Exhibition, Conferences and Cooperative 
Week Celebrations are being conducted. 
• 
TNCU collects 3%  and 2% of the net profit from profit making Cooperative 
Societies for Cooperative Research and Development Fund and Cooperative 
Education Fund respectively. 
• 
Out of these funds, loans / grants  are given for the development of 
Cooperative 
Research, 
Business 
development 
activities, 
Cooperative 
Education and  Training, Advertisement  and Propaganda work to  
Cooperative Societies. 
•

the Tamil Nadu Cooperative Union is conducting various training 
programmes through 24 Institutes of Cooperative Management, 2 Industrial 
Training Institutes and one Polytechnic College and Issue certificates to 
successful trainees.  It also runs one Arts and Science College at Dindigul 
District. 
• 
To create awareness among the general public about the functioning and 
services of Cooperatives, Member Education Programmes are conducted by 
District Cooperative Unions.

INTEGRATED COOPERATIVE DEVELOPMENT PROJECT 
The Integrated Cooperative Development Project was started with the aim 
of making cooperative institutions as economically stable institutions.It also 
prioritised improvement of infrastructure facilities of cooperatives. The National 
Cooperative Development Corporation used to provide financial assistance to 
Government of Tamil Nadu, in the form of loan for the project. In turn, the 
Government of Tamil Nadu offered assistance to Cooperatives in the form of 
Loan and Share Capital. In Tamil Nadu, this Project was commenced in 1989 
and was completed in 2023 in 32 districts with a financial assistance of 
Rs.821.75 Crore . The implementation of the scheme enabled the evolution of 
cooperative societies as multi-purpose institutions. It led to the increase of 
storage capacity, improvement in total business turnover, revival of dormant 
societies, construction of new godowns, repair works, human resource 
development and capacity building at the grass root level.

COOPERATIVE ELECTION 
 
In Tamil Nadu, Elections to the Cooperative Societies were conducted in the 
year 2013 for a total of 22,552 Cooperative Societies functioning under the control 
of Registrar of Cooperative Societies and 14 Functional Registrars and 2,04,889 
Board members were elected 
In April 2018, on completion of 5 years, the term of office of the members 
of the Cooperative Societies elected in 2013 had expired. Election Programme was 
issued for 18,805 Cooperative Societies in 4 phases under the control of the 
Registrar of Cooperative Societies and 14 Functional Registrars by the Tamil Nadu 
State Cooperative Societies Election Commission.  
Out of 18,805, elections were conducted to 18,249 Cooperative Societies 
and Board of Directors were elected and assumed charge. 
Presently there are around 23,016 Cooperative Societies functioning under 
the control of Registrar of Cooperative Societies and 14 Functional Registrars in 
Tamil Nadu. The 5 year tenure of the Board of Directors elected through the 
Enmasse Election 2018 expired . 
Meanwhile, the Hon'ble High Court of Madras, in its order dated 28.04.2023, 
in W.P.No.5171/2023 passed orders to rectify all the lapses and defects in the 
electoral rolls and gave a six-month period for rectification and then directed the 
Tamil Nadu State Co-operative Societies Election Commission to conduct the 
elections. Meanwhile another W.P.No.30700/2023 filed before Hon'ble High Court 
of Madras ordered that elections would be conducted on the basis of proper 
electoral rolls prepared and by adhering to the rules, in its order dated 
20.10.2023.  
Administrators have been appointed to the Cooperative societies where the 
5 year tenure of the elected board of members has expired to carry out 
administration.
