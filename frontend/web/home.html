Every answer is checked, twice

A public-services assistant with guardrails on the way in and on the way out. Personal details never reach the model, an agent cannot act without a person saying yes, and every request leaves a trace you can read afterwards.

How a request is checked, start to finish

One Supervisor Agent plans and delegates before anything reaches the model — GuardrailSupervisor.run() in guardrail_supervisor.py — routing to whichever of six specialist agents its own PLAN call actually names. Only once that layer is clear does the request reach the real tools: retrieval (search_documents, BM25 plus an optional local rerank) and the model itself. The reply then answers to a second, symmetric Output Supervisor Agent — the same kind of plan-then-check pass, this time against what came back: PII, safety, policy, grounding, and citations against what was actually retrieved. Only REJECT ever routes to a person; everything ALLOW reaches becomes the response. What people ask, and what happens

Four real turns through the guardrail stack — none of these are mock-ups; each one is a request that was actually run.

My name is Ravi Kumar and my phone number is 044-2534 0508 — how do I become a cooperative member?

Masking is not refusing. The name and phone number are detected and masked, and the request carries on — answered correctly from the Citizen Charter: own agricultural land in the society's area, then pay the admission fee and share capital.

What are the duties of citizens under the cooperative movement?

The corpus indexes that heading but retrieval only surfaces its table-of-contents entry, not the page it points to. The answer says so and asks for the excerpt, rather than inventing a duty that sounds plausible.
